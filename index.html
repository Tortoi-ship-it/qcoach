QCoach Quick Guide & Key Adjustments

Everything you need to start playing and customize your practice · Press ? anytime

🚀 1. Quick Start — 4 Steps to Play

Start here
1. Tune Your Ukulele

Open the Tuner tab and tap 🎙️ Start Microphone Tuner to tune G4–C4–E4–A4 (locks green within ±3 cents), or play reference tones.

2. Choose or Add a Song

Browse 175+ built-in arrangements in Songs, or tap ➕ Add to search by name, paste a YouTube link, or upload a PDF/MP3/MP4.

3. Press ▶ PLAY on Stage

A 3–2–1 countdown gives you 3 seconds to place your fingers on the first chord, then synced Fretboard, Lyrics & Pitch Contour guide you bar by bar.

4. Follow the Beat LEDs

Land cleanly on 1 · LAND (green). When 4& · LIFT! flashes red, lift your fingers to switch smoothly to the UP NEXT chord.

🎸 2. Basic Stage Controls

🎸 Chord / 🎼 Melody

Switch between Chord View (full fretboard chord diagrams & strumming) and Melody View (single-note ukulele tabs shown as String:Fret).

Strum / Pick Strip

Follow the live ↓ / ↑ strum arrows in the top beat bar (read-only so you never edit by accident). Tap 🎸 Strum / 🤌 Pick or open ⋮ Settings to change patterns.

🔊 Audition & Bars

Click 🔊 Strum / Pluck on the current or UP NEXT card to preview its sound. Tap any bar (B1, B2…) in the progression strip to jump directly to that measure.

Voice Separation

Use ▶ YouTube Vocal Sync at the bottom of Stage to play the real singer in sync with your chords, or pick any of the 4 offline Stage Audio Modes above the Pitch Contour: 🎵 Song, 🎤 Vocals, 🎸 Karaoke (ukulele backing without vocals), or 🥁 Beat Only (metronome click with no music accompaniment).

🎛️ 3. Key Adjustments (Bottom Dock & ⋮ Settings)

Customize to your pace
Tempo, Speed & Auto-Ramp (Bottom Dock)

Tap -5 / +5 BPM or 60% · 75% · 100% to slow down tricky passages. Enable 📈 Ramp to automatically add +5 BPM after every 2 loops.

Key Transpose & Meter (⋮ Settings → 3)

Transpose any song -6 to +6 semitones to match your voice, change meter (3/4, 4/4, 6/8), loop a specific Practice Section, or edit any bar’s chord.

Skill Tier & Voice Coach (⋮ Settings → 1)

Switch between Beginner (open chords), Intermediate, and Expert voicings. Toggle spoken Voice Coach cues, TTS Lyrics, or High-Neck voicings.

3-Channel Mixer & Beat-1 Sync (⋮ Settings → 4)

Independently adjust or mute Beat Click, Ukulele/Backing, and Vocals. Use -50ms / +50ms nudges to fine-tune Beat 1 alignment on uploaded MP3s.

🌓 Display Theme (Header Icon or ⋮ Settings)

Defaults to Bright. Tap the ☀️ header button or open ⋮ Settings anytime to switch to Gray Tone, Dark, or Auto (Phone).

📚 4. Songs, Imports, Tuner & Backups

➕ Add & Lyrics

Search and pick songs in Songs. Add new songs (PDF/MP3/MP4, YouTube, or Manual Form) or batch ✨ Auto-Fill in ⋮ Settings → 2. On Stage, tap the small ● Auto-Fill This Song dot at the bottom to complete lyrics & bars for the currently playing song.

🎙️ Tuner Tab

Uses real-time microphone pitch detection for Concert Ukulele (G4 · C4 · E4 · A4). If no microphone is active, use the built-in string buttons and tuning peg slider to practice tuning by ear.

☁️ Backup & Sync

In ⋮ Settings → 5, export/restore .qcoach.json backups, save a standalone HTML copy with your entire library embedded, or sync to Google Drive.
